Why “Bananen” can be recognized but not cause an interruption

Keyword matching and speaker authorization are separate. An agreed literal rule needs no new LLM call after it is installed, but it still requires a sufficiently confident current speaker. Proactive interjections require a teacher cosine of at least 0.55, stable evidence, plus short and long student checks. This is deliberately stricter than the ordinary 0.32 utterance setting. Echo and short phonetic windows can weaken student evidence. Rules also have a four-second rearm interval and require fresh words after the previous reply; they do not fire repeatedly on an old transcript.

Do not interpret a passing banana test as proof that interruption always works. The trace includes periods of correctly transcribed words rejected by identity checks. The separate brief user “Stop” browser failure remains open. No blanket threshold reduction was made: it could admit unrelated background speakers.
