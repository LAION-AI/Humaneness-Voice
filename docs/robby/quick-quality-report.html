Robby: better short reactions, with measured limitations

The existing local Nemotron Bolt E4B writes up to six words while Qwen produces the full answer. This study tests the words, their context and the handoff. It is not a human listening panel. All inputs are authored synthetic conversations.

Decision: retain low temperature (0.35), use small tone-specific prompt examples, preserve joke follow-ups, suppress repeated openings and unsupported action promises, and keep the complete main answer. Playful replies receive a playful TTS cue; serious replies use soft delivery. Examples guide the model; no phrase is selected directly for playback, and there is no extra online judge or retry.

The trade-off is explicit: engagement and relevance improved on the model rating; naturalness decreased slightly. Some phrases such as “Das lohnt einen Blick” remain generic. Several joke openings are charming, but not every attempt is funny. The evaluator sometimes prefers an action promise that our policy disallows; ratings do not override the runtime checks.

Method and changes

Forty fixed situations, with three seeds per eligible situation (110 baseline rows; 106 selected rows, including explicit skips). Twenty-eight were development situations; twelve were reserved until selecting a candidate. The first held-out pass exposed an English-language parsing bug. That bug and a compound-joke follow-up bug were repaired; the final rerun is therefore a regression evaluation, not untouched held-out evidence. Eight development prompt variants including baseline were explored; all receipts remain below.

  • Increasing temperature or asking for more imaginative wording often produced unnatural word combinations or questions. Those variants were rejected.
  • Short tone hints cover play, jokes, distress, support, kindness, achievements, corrections, casual chat and curiosity. They are keyword heuristics, not psychological diagnoses.
  • Recent assistant openings are supplied as examples to avoid. Exact repeated openings are suppressed without a second model call.
  • Urgent help, simple arithmetic, exact-output requests, poems and Stop skip the optional preface. Invalid output never blocks the full answer.
  • “Katzenwitz”, “Pinguinwitz” and “Und noch einen” preserve the joke task. Explicit “Please speak English” works for both models.
  • Duplicate short condolences at the handoff are removed, including “Oh, das tut mir so, so sehr leid.” Advice or substantive sentences are preserved.
