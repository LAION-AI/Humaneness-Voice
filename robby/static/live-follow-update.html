Root causes and repairs

  • The scheduler advertised 320 ms but throttled proactive checks to 640 ms. It now evaluates every 320-ms audio hop. This is a scheduling interval, NOT a promise of a model/API response or audible speech within 320 ms.
  • A fired rule remained latched until silence. Fresh post-playback mentions can now re-arm it after four seconds, even during continuous speech. Old trigger words in cumulative ASR do not by themselves re-arm a rule.
  • An unsuccessful stop-identity check could permanently consume an unchanged ASR candidate. It now retries while the audio remains fresh.
  • The first repeated-topic replay exposed a Laya false positive: ordinary continuing speech canceled the interjection as though it were Stop. Incoming semantic checks now require a stop-related cue; ambiguous requests use Luna, not repeated uncalibrated Laya guesses. This does not authorize anyone without the existing acoustic verification.
  • Literal rules preempt a pending contextual API request. There is one outstanding decision task and no queue of stale decisions. Unchanged semantic text reuses its decision; speaker/visual admission remains fresh.
  • A native teacher/echo check previously awaited inside the microphone hop. It is now a single-flight background task with reply-ID and identity-epoch rechecks. The shared GPU lock prioritizes waiting live encoder hops over another ASR decode; an in-flight decode cannot be preempted.
  • Diarization can split a continuous monologue after an interjection. A bounded, same-person, same-literal-rule tail no longer produces an extra ordinary reply after the pause. Explicit cancellation and new requests are not swallowed.
  • A stopped camera presentation timestamp could make current landmarks appear stale. Fresh captured frames now get monotonic bounded timestamps when presentation callbacks stop advancing. The display reports actual pan/tilt or manual-override status.
  • Resume eye contact exits frozen calibration, clears manual/tour overrides, recenters the inspection camera and resumes camera tracking. Optional gallery/recognition UI failures no longer prevent single-face gaze updates.

Useful interjections versus exact agreements

The new default-on, separately switchable useful-interjection option asks Luna to decide from interim words and recent conversation. It may briefly say “Darf ich kurz einhaken?” / “May I jump in briefly?” when invited or when intervention is clearly useful. It does not invent a new long answer. A 12-second cooldown prevents chatter. Ordinary questions, requests to finish, uncertainty and ambiguous identities should be left alone. The main interruption checkbox disables both proactive paths.

Laya remains available for incoming Stop decisions. Its contextual paired classifier returned near-chance scores on our invitation controls, so it was NOT made more permissive. Contextual decisions use the existing Luna provider instead; exact agreed topic rules use local keywords, without another LLM call. This is slower for open-ended meaning decisions.
