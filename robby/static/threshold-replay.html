Voice-match threshold replay

The current user-requested normal-turn default is 0.32, previously 0.45. This permissive setting is not human-calibrated and can accept unrelated voices. The visible slider supports 0.30–0.90; Reset adopts 0.32 while custom saved settings are preserved. Fast-cue strictness is separate.

The configured normal cosine also applies when a known person gets a new diarization channel; that path still requires a 0.12 rival gap and an independently agreeing strong fast cue below 0.50. Ordinary turns retain the 0.08 gap. View consistency, transcript provenance and playback-echo rejection remain enabled. Voice interruptions have additional current-addressee checks; their short-command experiments are documented separately in the worklog.

The existing default combines streaming Nemotron diarization, Nemotron-feature speaker prediction and full-sentence ECAPA references. Diarization channel numbers are session-local, not persistent identities. Voiceprints stay in RAM. Method and limitations

Re-score stored ECAPA cosines with fixed 0.08 rival margin. No ASR, fast-cue, view-agreement, energy, playback or timing gates are simulated.

Three development synthetic identities, repeated prompts, correlated noise conditions and one unrelated background identity. Genuine fixed-length references and multi-duration background galleries differ. Not held-out real-human validation or an end-to-end success rate.
