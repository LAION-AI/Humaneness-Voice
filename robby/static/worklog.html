Eye-contact formula

Assume a 65° horizontal field of view and a 63-mm eye baseline. Correct the eye-pair span for image aspect and relative z; estimate and clamp distance Z to 0.22–2.8 m. Let rayX/rayY be the eye-midpoint camera rays and g=Z/(Z+0.08). Fit each tangent angle with features [1, g·rayX/0.35, g·rayY/0.25, 0.35·(1/Z−1/0.65)]. Ridge regularization keeps the uncalibrated geometry as a prior, particularly for depth with few examples. The 80-mm pivot setback, IPD and FOV are explicit heuristics, not measured camera intrinsics. Calibration learns the residual offsets/coupling from your subjective confirmations.

The tracker does not infer where your own eyes are looking: it directs Robby towards their position. Pure roll and nose movement do not drive his head. Camera and calibration geometry stay in this browser; only typed/transcribed conversation text reaches the configured Luna API.

A genuine one-second acoustic pause, changed candidate or six seconds without a probe resets this state. The background-stop negative exposed a separate bypass: a queued, already-transcribed stop used ordinary full-turn identity admission and called BARGE directly. At the intentionally permissive 0.32 normal-turn threshold this could admit a background stop even though the dedicated interruption verifier had rejected it.
