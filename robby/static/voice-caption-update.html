How it works

Speech pause → ASR and GPU caption in parallel → speaker admission → ready hint or omission → LLM → streaming TTS

The caption job starts on the same finalized audio snapshot as ASR. Its result belongs to that turn only. When the turn is accepted, Robby uses an already-completed result or skips it: a late result adds no awaited delay before the response model. The worker analyzes at most the last eight seconds, stores no microphone files and loads no second ASR or generative caption model.

The pinned Humaneness Ears Medium encoder supplies scores. The pinned Procedural Voice Captions rules use their measured speech-reference normalization. Robby limits the text to prosody and ordinary emotional cues, omitting demographic, identity, sincerity and quality judgments.

The LLM is told that these are uncertain acoustic estimates, not facts about feelings or intentions. The person's actual words take priority. Noise, other speakers and acting can mislead the model. A caption never authorizes a speaker switch, an interruption or ignoring a request.

Controls and real integration tests

Under Speech pause & interruption options, Use uncertain tone-of-voice hints is independently switchable and remembered. A short caption/status and its timing are visible. Typed input receives no guessed voice tone. The camera remains manually available. Nemotron still handles live previews, speaker features and interruption candidates; Parakeet produces the final transcript.
