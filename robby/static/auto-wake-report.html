Automatic listening and eye contact

Open Robby · Wake mode report · Implementation and reproduction

What changes

On a new or migrated browser, Robby starts its microphone and camera automatically when server readiness and browser permissions allow. It waits for “Hey Robby”, “Hi Robbie”, “Hallo Robbi” and equivalent forms. Camera landmarks drive position-based gaze following in a browser CPU worker. Webcam Face ID and face-based speech filtering remain off.

The current speaker can follow up for ten seconds after Robby finishes actual playback, even after a long answer. Uncertain/different voices still need the wake phrase. Turn Robby off stops microphone, camera and playback; the off choice persists. Microphone, camera and autoplay permission decisions remain under browser control.

Limits and reproduction

Microphone and camera were browser fake devices. Gaze-direction logic used synthetic eye landmarks; it is not a human eye-contact accuracy measurement. Recognition and speaker verification are approximate, especially for short words or overlap. Some browsers require an explicit microphone/camera grant and an activation click before sound may play; Robby shows the enable control when blocked.
